**Checked/attempted:** November 3, 2026. **Scope:** all 24 destinations in the existing country catalog. **Audience:** U.S. passport holders. **Verification status:** source discovery and selected-content review; not completed country fact verification.

This supplement addresses the request to find sources where coverage is lacking. It adds targeted source candidates beside existing bank leads, records access failures and misleading redirects, and identifies the research question each source should answer. It does not replace or rewrite the country drafts. Both Japan drafts remain unchanged.

See the [collection and accuracy review](VERIFICATION_REPORT.md) for the completed source-inventory audit, extraction totals and unresolved accuracy issues.

## What was missing

- **Medicines:** CDC covers health recommendations, but not all destination import permissions. Added destination health/drug/customs authorities where found. Incoming traveller rules must be separated from outgoing-resident advice and postal imports.
- **Weather:** Added national meteorological or hazard-service entry points. Some return shells or fail. Seasonal calendars and exact-date forecasts still need separate, region-specific extraction.
- **Connectivity:** Added destination-specific Airalo and Holafly product pages. These provide purchase and package leads, not a guarantee of coverage everywhere. The Holafly Iraq redirect is rejected as country evidence.
- **Transport apps:** Added operator/provider pages for SUBE, ÖBB, SNCB, DB, Yassir and other gaps. Some fail or redirect, so platform downloads and geographic scope remain review items.
- **Customs and long stays:** Added EU/UK primary rules and destination-specific leads. EU, Schengen, UK and national residence rules must remain distinct.
- **Electricity:** Added destination electrical-safety references. Adapt their UK-audience advice for U.S. devices and resolve conflicting technical sources.

## High-risk review queue

1. **Entry and stay permissions:** Check actual U.S.-passport categories, work/study exclusions, dated waivers, arrival forms, EES/ETIAS and extension/residence paths. UK and Panama can have tourist allowances longer than 90 days; do not force all countries into a 90-day legal threshold.
2. **Qatar:** The State page gives conflicting passport validity periods. Resolve with MOI before drafting a definitive requirement.
3. **Cash:** Check destination customs thresholds, inclusive/exclusive wording, monetary instruments, inward/outward direction and declaration forms. Several State summaries use “maximum” for a declaration rule. Korea’s summary says no declaration is needed and requires local customs review.
4. **Medicines:** Confirm ingredients, quantity, documents and permits with the destination authority. Egypt outbound advice is not inbound permission. Incoming medicine rules remain especially weak for Algeria, Egypt, Iraq, Panama, Paraguay and Senegal.
5. **Iraq and regional risk:** Recheck current U.S. advisory, embassy service availability and federal/Kurdistan permissions; source availability or a commercial product page does not establish safe travel.
6. **Emergency help:** Reopen the exact local emergency and consular contact sections. A reachable homepage alone is not number verification.
