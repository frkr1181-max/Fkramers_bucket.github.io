## 1. Entry, passport, and stay type

- **Paid work needs the correct status** — A short visitor stay does not authorize paid work. Confirm the required Japanese visa and residence status with the responsible embassy or consulate before travel.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.* **Applies to:** purpose: work.
- **Passport and permission checks** — Carry a passport valid throughout the visit with a blank stamp page. A passport previously reported lost or stolen cannot be reused. Work and long-stay plans require the appropriate immigration permission.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*
- **Short tourist stays** — U.S. short-term tourism is visa-exempt for up to 90 days under Japanese visitor rules. Paid work is outside that visitor permission. Check the exact conditions with Japanese immigration.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.* **Applies to:** purpose: tourism; trip length: 1–90 days.
- **Stays beyond the short-visit allowance** — For a stay over 90 days, confirm the appropriate Japanese visa and residence status for the purpose. MOFA says a Certificate of Eligibility is normally obtained before the visa application; it is not a guarantee of visa issuance.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.* **Applies to:** trip length: 91 days or longer.

- Sources: [Japan Ministry of Foreign Affairs — Visa information](https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/index.html) (checked 2026-11-05); [U.S. Department of State — Japan: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/japan.html) (checked 2026-11-05); [Embassy of Japan in the United States — Visa frequently asked questions](https://www.us.emb-japan.go.jp/itpr_en/visa-faq.html) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 2. Getting there and border arrival

- **Customs and arrival preparation** — Japan Customs describes passenger clearance and declarations. Check restrictions before packing food, plants, animal products, medicines, or other controlled goods. Electronic procedures do not replace the required passport or immigration permission.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*
- **Narita Airport rail choices** — Narita Airport lists JR Narita Express and Keisei services. Confirm the train, terminal station, ticket, and onward route before boarding. These are Narita-area options, not arrival instructions for every Japanese airport.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Japan Customs — Customs and arrival preparation](https://www.customs.go.jp/english/summary/passenger.htm) (checked 2026-11-05); [Narita International Airport — Narita Airport rail choices](https://www.narita-airport.jp/en/access/train/) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 3. Getting around the country

- **Countrywide transport planning** — Japan's official tourism guidance covers rail, buses, rental cars, and regional transport. Coverage and tickets differ by operator. A rail pass should be checked against the exact route before purchase.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Japan National Tourism Organization — Countrywide transport planning](https://www.japan.travel/en/plan/getting-around/) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 4. Currency, payments, and customs cash declaration

- **Cash declaration is reporting, not a spending limit** — Japan Customs requires reporting covered cash and other payment instruments above one million yen equivalent when entering or leaving Japan. Check the included items and special rules on the Customs page; this is not a blanket maximum amount you may carry.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*
- **Currency and payment planning** — Japanese yen (JPY). Many businesses accept cards, but some take cash only. Compatible ATMs are available; check withdrawal and exchange fees.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Japan Customs — Cash declaration is reporting, not a spending limit](https://www.customs.go.jp/english/summary/passenger.htm) (checked 2026-11-05); [U.S. Department of State — Japan: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/japan.html) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 6. Health, care, and medicine

- **Check each medicine before packing** — A U.S. prescription does not itself grant import permission. Japan's rules depend on the ingredient, amount, and medicine classification; some cases require advance Import Confirmation or other authorization. Check MHLW's instructions and ask about the exact medicine before departure.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*
- **Health preparation for your itinerary** — Discuss Japanese-encephalitis vaccination for longer stays or higher-risk rural/outdoor exposure; it is not a blanket recommendation for every short urban trip. Review routine vaccines, including measles protection. Any global CDC notice is not by itself a country-specific outbreak report. Arrange advice before travel; this summary does not prescribe treatment.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Japan Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare — Check each medicine before packing](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/kenkou_iryou/iyakuhin/kojinyunyu/topics/tp010401-1_00001.html) (checked 2026-11-05); [U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention — Japan: Traveler View](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/destinations/traveler/none/japan) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 7. Local culture and respectful travel

- **Shoes and venue-specific etiquette** — Removing shoes is customary in some traditional accommodation and indoor spaces; follow the entrance instructions and keep outdoor shoes off tatami mats. Rules vary by venue. Ask staff rather than treating every custom as a national law.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Japan National Tourism Organization — Shoes and venue-specific etiquette](https://www.japan.travel/en/guide/understanding-and-mastering-japanese-manners-and-etiquette/) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 8. Phones, internet, and staying connected

- **Airport Wi-Fi and connection planning** — JNTO describes free Wi-Fi at major international airports. Follow the particular airport's connection instructions. For travel away from hotspots, compare the U.S. carrier's roaming terms with a compatible local service before departure.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Japan National Tourism Organization — Airport Wi-Fi and connection planning](https://www.japan.travel/en/plan/wifi-and-connectivity/) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 9. Electricity, plugs, and device preparation

- **Japan voltage and regional frequency** — Japan uses 100 V electricity. Frequency is 50 Hz in the east and 60 Hz in the west. Check device voltage, frequency, plug fit, and grounding; a plug adapter does not convert the supply. Follow the manufacturer's instructions.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Japan National Tourism Organization — Japan voltage and regional frequency](https://www.japan.travel/en/plan/plug-and-electricity/) (checked 2026-11-05).
