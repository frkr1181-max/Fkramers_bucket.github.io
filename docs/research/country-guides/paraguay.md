# Paraguay country guide

**Audience:** U.S. passport holders.  
**Facts checked:** 2026-11-05; individual dates appear with each fact.  
**Status:** Published facts, arranged in Japan’s ten-section guide format.  
**Formatted:** November 5, 2026.

Requirements can depend on trip purpose, length of stay, route, and personal circumstances. The conditions below identify facts that apply to a particular trip type. Regional, airport, and provider examples retain their stated scope. This collection does not provide a live forecast, exchange rate, or disruption feed.

## 1. Entry, passport, and stay type

- **Passport and permission checks** — Carry a passport with six months left after arrival and a blank page. Yellow-fever proof can depend on the country traveled from; check the complete route.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*
- **Short tourist stays** — The checked U.S. guidance permits visa-free visits of up to 90 days. Confirm the current U.S.-passport exemption with Paraguayan immigration before travel; it does not authorize employment.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.* **Applies to:** purpose: tourism; trip length: 1–90 days.

- Sources: [U.S. Department of State — Paraguay: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/paraguay.html) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 2. Getting there and border arrival

## 3. Getting around the country

- **Asunción metropolitan bus cards** — The transport ministry describes electronic fare cards, including Jaha and Más, for the Asunción metropolitan bus system. Check current card and service conditions; this is not a claim about every bus in Paraguay.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Paraguay Ministry of Public Works and Communications — Asunción metropolitan bus cards](https://mopc.gov.py/viceministerios/viceministerio-de-transporte/preguntas-frecuentes/) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 4. Currency, payments, and customs cash declaration

- **Currency and payment planning** — Paraguayan guaraní (PYG). Large urban businesses often take cards; many small and rural businesses require cash.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [U.S. Department of State — Paraguay: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/paraguay.html) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 5. Safety, weather, and disruptions

- **U.S. advisory and regional precautions** — The U.S. advisory is Level 1 nationally. Alto Paraná, Amambay, Canindeyú, Concepción, and San Pedro have increased crime warnings. Advisory checked November 5, 2026; recheck before departure.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [U.S. Department of State — Paraguay: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/paraguay.html) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 6. Health, care, and medicine

- **Health preparation for your itinerary** — Discuss yellow-fever vaccination for eligible travelers. CDC distinguishes visits limited to Asunción from other itineraries, and entry proof depends on prior travel. Review routine vaccines, including measles protection. Any global CDC notice is not by itself a country-specific outbreak report. Arrange advice before travel; this summary does not prescribe treatment.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention — Paraguay: Traveler View](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/destinations/traveler/none/paraguay) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 7. Local culture and respectful travel

- **Spanish and Guaraní** — Spanish and Guaraní are Paraguay's official languages. The tourism authority also describes everyday use of Jopará, a mix of the two. Do not assume that one language preference applies to everyone.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [SENATUR — Spanish and Guaraní](https://visitparaguay.travel/planea-tu-viaje) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 8. Phones, internet, and staying connected

- **Follow Tigo’s passport activation steps** — Tigo lists the United States among the countries whose travelers can activate its self-activation SIM using a passport or accepted foreign document. Use the official activation site and follow the instructions for that document type. Have your passport and phone ready, and ask an official shop for help if activation fails. Check the current package, coverage, calls, data, and validity before adding credit; passport acceptance does not make every plan or phone compatible.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Tigo Paraguay — How to activate a self-activation SIM](https://ayuda.tigo.com.py/hc/centro-de-ayuda/articles/3144175190491411) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 9. Electricity, plugs, and device preparation

- **Plugs, supply, and device compatibility** — Electrical Safety First lists Type C plugs, 220 V, and 50 Hz. Check each device's input label and the actual outlet. A plug adapter changes fit, not voltage. A 120 V-only appliance needs manufacturer-approved conversion or should be left at home.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Electrical Safety First — Paraguay: travel adaptors](https://www.electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk/safety-advice/travel-advice/paraguay/) (checked 2026-11-05).
