# Qatar country guide

**Audience:** U.S. passport holders.  
**Facts checked:** 2026-11-05; individual dates appear with each fact.  
**Status:** Published facts, arranged in Japan’s ten-section guide format.  
**Formatted:** November 5, 2026.

Requirements can depend on trip purpose, length of stay, route, and personal circumstances. The conditions below identify facts that apply to a particular trip type. Regional, airport, and provider examples retain their stated scope. This collection does not provide a live forecast, exchange rate, or disruption feed.

## 1. Entry, passport, and stay type

- **Passport and permission checks** — Confirm entry conditions with Qatar's immigration authority before departure. The checked U.S. page gives conflicting passport-validity periods. Do not treat one of those periods, a visa fee, or a visa duration as confirmed for your case.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [U.S. Department of State — Qatar: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/qatar.html) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 2. Getting there and border arrival

- **Check personal baggage and cash declaration rules** — Qatar Customs exempts qualifying personal belongings and gifts only when they are personal, non-commercial, and within the stated value conditions; restricted goods remain subject to other laws. A separate declaration is required when carrying currency, bearer instruments, precious metals, or stones worth QAR 50,000 or more (or equivalent). Check the official rules before packing and ask Customs about anything unclear.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Qatar General Authority of Customs — Materials authorized to enter and passenger exemptions](https://www.customs.gov.qa/English/PassengersIndividualsPortal/Pages/TravelersDeclaration.aspx) (checked 2026-11-05); [Qatar General Authority of Customs — Customs declaration of money transferred across borders](https://customs.gov.qa/English/PassengersIndividualsPortal/Pages/TravelersExemption.aspx) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 3. Getting around the country

- **Separate Doha transport from trips farther afield** — Doha has metro, bus, and taxi options, but the same network does not reach every destination in Qatar. Use Qatar Rail’s information for metro journeys and the Karwa apps for the bus or taxi service you need. Check the current route, operating hours, and payment method before leaving. Some metro-connection services operate only in defined areas, so confirm the pickup point rather than assuming door-to-door coverage.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Qatar Tourism — Getting around in Qatar](https://visitqatar.com/intl-en/plan-your-trip/getting-around) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 4. Currency, payments, and customs cash declaration

- **Currency and payment planning** — Qatari riyal (QAR). Card payments are widely used; ATMs are available in Doha. Check conversion and withdrawal charges.

## 6. Health, care, and medicine

- **Health preparation for your itinerary** — Discuss hepatitis A and other personal vaccine needs with a clinician. Check medicine-import permission separately before packing prescriptions. Review routine vaccines, including measles protection. Any global CDC notice is not by itself a country-specific outbreak report. Arrange advice before travel; this summary does not prescribe treatment.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention — Qatar: Traveler View](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/destinations/traveler/none/qatar) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 7. Local culture and respectful travel

- **Clothing as a respectful custom** — Visit Qatar recommends avoiding revealing public clothing and keeping shoulders and knees covered. This is the tourism authority's cultural advice; individual venues and legal restrictions may impose other requirements.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Qatar Tourism — Clothing as a respectful custom](https://visitqatar.com/intl-en/plan-your-trip/travel-tips) (checked 2026-11-05).

  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

## 9. Electricity, plugs, and device preparation

- **Plugs, supply, and device compatibility** — Electrical Safety First lists Type G plugs, 240 V, and 50 Hz. Check each device's input label and the actual outlet. A plug adapter changes fit, not voltage. A 120 V-only appliance needs manufacturer-approved conversion or should be left at home.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Electrical Safety First — Qatar: travel adaptors](https://www.electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk/safety-advice/travel-advice/qatar/) (checked 2026-11-05).
