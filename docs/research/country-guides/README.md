# Country guides

The 24 guides below use the same ten numbered topics as the Japan guide, with practical fact bullets followed by sources for each section. They contain all 263 published facts available in Supabase as of November 5, 2026. England and Scotland are separate destinations and share the GB country code; the 24 destinations represent 23 country codes.

Each guide preserves the published wording, citations, verification dates, trip-purpose conditions, stay-length limits, and local scope. Formatting a fact does not change its verification date. This collection supplements the original source-bank drafts and the historical [Japan review draft](../JAPAN_REVIEW_DRAFT.md).

## Shared format

1. Entry, passport, and stay type
2. Getting there and border arrival
3. Getting around the country
4. Currency, payments, and customs cash declaration
5. Safety, weather, and disruptions
6. Health, care, and medicine
7. Local culture and respectful travel
8. Phones, internet, and staying connected
9. Electricity, plugs, and device preparation
10. Emergency help and U.S. consular support

Every section contains at least one published fact. The guides retain the limits of the underlying research; they are not exhaustive guides or live travel feeds. Source-bank notes that have not been approved remain in [country-drafts](../country-drafts/README.md).
