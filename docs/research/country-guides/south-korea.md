## 1. Entry, passport, and stay type

- **Short visit for tourism or qualifying business** — U.S. citizens may visit visa-free for tourism or qualifying business for up to 90 days with a valid U.S. passport. The U.S. State Department says K-ETA is exempt through December 31, 2026 and required from January 1, 2027 for this visa-waiver route. Confirm the rule for your travel date and activity with Korean authorities before departure.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-03.* **Applies to:** purpose: tourism, business; trip length: 1–90 days.
- **Stays longer than 90 days** — A stay longer than 90 days requires the visa that matches the reason for travel. Work, teaching, study, and residence need the correct visa even when a traveler plans a shorter stay. Apply through the official Korean visa system or the Korean consular office responsible for your U.S. residence; do not assume a short-visit entry can be extended or changed in Korea.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-03.* **Applies to:** trip length: 91 days or longer.
- **Work or study needs the right visa** — If you plan to work or study, check the visa required for that activity before travel. A visa-free short visit does not grant permission to work, teach, or study. Confirm the requirements for your exact activity with the Korean embassy or consulate serving your U.S. residence.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-03.* **Applies to:** purpose: study, work.

- Sources: [U.S. Department of State — South Korea Travel Advisory and entry information](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/south-korea.html) (checked 2026-11-03); [Korea Ministry of Justice, K-ETA — Notice on Extension of K-ETA Temporary Exemption](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/board/viewboarddetail.do?bbsSn=299707&esntlPnotiMtYn=Y&locale=EN) (checked 2026-11-03); [Korea Ministry of Justice — Korea Visa Portal](https://www.visa.go.kr/) (checked 2026-11-03).

## 2. Getting there and border arrival

- **Arrival card, immigration, and customs** — Most foreign visitors without a valid K-ETA must submit an arrival card. Korea’s free e-Arrival Card can be submitted during the 72 hours before arrival; valid K-ETA holders and some other groups are exempt. Follow airport signs for immigration, baggage, quarantine when applicable, and customs. Customs rules apply to goods and cash; cash declaration guidance is under review because official Korean and U.S. sources disagree.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-03.*

- Sources: [Korea Ministry of Justice — e-Arrival Card eligibility and guide](https://www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr/portal/guide/eacTargetGuide.do?locale=EN) (checked 2026-11-03); [Incheon International Airport — Immigration and e-Arrival Card](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1448/subview.do) (checked 2026-11-03); [Korea Customs Service — Declaration of Foreign Currency](https://www.customs.go.kr/english/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=5500&mi=10800) (checked 2026-11-04); [U.S. Department of State — South Korea Travel Advisory — currency on entry and exit](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/south-korea.html) (checked 2026-11-04).

## 3. Getting around the country

- **Country transport and navigation** — Intercity trains, express and local buses, domestic flights, ferries, metro, and taxis are common options. Services, schedules, payments, and accessibility vary by operator and region; rural and island routes can be less frequent. Korea Tourism Organization lists NAVER Map and KakaoMap for route search, KORAIL+ for KORAIL trains, and k.ride for visitor taxi booking. Check current coverage and payment support in each official app or provider page.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-03.*

- Sources: [Korea Tourism Organization — International visitors’ transportation guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/public/contents/travel/KoreaTransportationGuide_enu.pdf) (checked 2026-11-03); [Korea Tourism Organization — Ride-hailing Apps in Korea](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=197907) (checked 2026-11-03).

## 4. Currency, payments, and customs cash declaration

- **Won and checking cash-declaration rules** — South Korea uses the won (KRW). Official cash guidance disagrees: Korea Customs describes a declaration requirement above US$10,000 equivalent in covered payment instruments, while the checked U.S. country summary says no declaration is needed. Contact Korea Customs before carrying a large amount and confirm the reporting process for your direction of travel. This fact does not resolve the disagreement or state that cash is exempt.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Korea Tourism Organization — Korean currency and exchange information](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?menuSn=374&vcontsId=140040) (checked 2026-11-05); [Korea Customs Service — Declaration of foreign currency](https://www.customs.go.kr/english/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=5500&mi=10800) (checked 2026-11-05); [U.S. Department of State — South Korea: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/south-korea.html) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 5. Safety, weather, and disruptions

- **Current advisory, laws, and weather risks** — The U.S. State Department lists South Korea at Level 1, Exercise normal precautions (issued May 28, 2025; checked November 3, 2026). It warns that demonstrations can disrupt services and that tensions on the Korean Peninsula can rise. Avoid demonstrations and follow local alerts. Drug laws are strict, and some U.S. prescriptions are restricted. Heavy rain and flooding can occur in summer; typhoons may affect the country from May to November. Recheck the advisory and forecasts close to travel.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-03.*

- Sources: [U.S. Department of State — South Korea Travel Advisory](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/south-korea.html) (checked 2026-11-03); [Korea Tourism Organization — Climate & Seasons](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?vcontsId=140636) (checked 2026-11-03); [Korea Meteorological Administration — Current Severe Weather Status](https://www.weather.go.kr/neng/forecast/warning.do) (checked 2026-11-04).

## 6. Health, care, and medicine

- **CDC vaccines, regional malaria, and care costs** — Check the CDC South Korea Traveler View and speak with a clinician about vaccines and medicines before travel. CDC recommends hepatitis A vaccine for unvaccinated travelers and identifies malaria risk in some rural northern parts of Incheon, Gangwon, and Gyeonggi provinces, including near the DMZ, during March–December. The malaria advice is regional, not countrywide. U.S. insurance may not cover care abroad; check coverage and consider medical evacuation insurance. Review medicine import rules before packing prescriptions.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-03.*

- Sources: [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention — South Korea Traveler View](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/destinations/traveler/none/south-korea) (checked 2026-11-03); [U.S. Department of State — South Korea Travel Advisory, health and insurance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/south-korea.html) (checked 2026-11-03); [Ministry of Food and Drug Safety — Manual on Permit to Bring in Narcotics to Korea](https://www.mfds.go.kr/eng/brd/m_60/view.do?seq=72497) (checked 2026-11-04).

## 7. Local culture and respectful travel

- **Dining and respectful conduct** — Tipping is generally not expected in restaurants. Follow posted rules at homes, temples, and other venues, including shoe removal and photography guidance. Customs differ by setting; observe local signs and ask when unsure.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-03.*

- Sources: [Korea Tourism Organization — About Korean Food and restaurant guidance](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?menuSn=903&vcontsId=140727) (checked 2026-11-03).

## 8. Phones, internet, and staying connected

- **SIM, eSIM, and roaming options** — Ask your U.S. carrier about South Korea roaming, costs, device compatibility, and coverage. Local prepaid SIM and eSIM options are available from carriers including KT and LG U+; check the current plan, coverage, data and voice limits, device compatibility, and activation rules with the provider. Coverage can vary by location, so do not assume service everywhere.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-03.*

- Sources: [KT — KT tourist eSIM](https://m.roaming.kt.com/eng/esim) (checked 2026-11-03); [LG U+ — LG U+ Korea eSIM](https://www.lguplus.com/korea-sim/eng/pc/product/esim?tab=data) (checked 2026-11-03); [Korea Tourism Organization — Electricity & Communications](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140041) (checked 2026-11-03).

## 9. Electricity, plugs, and device preparation

- **Voltage, frequency, and plug adapter** — South Korea’s standard supply is 220 volts at 60 hertz, with two round-pin outlets common. U.S. travelers generally need a plug adapter. A plug adapter does not change voltage: check each device label, and use a voltage converter or leave behind any device that is not rated for 220 V.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-03.*

- Sources: [Korea Tourism Organization — Electricity & Communications](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=140041) (checked 2026-11-03); [World Standards — Plug, socket and voltage by country: South Korea](https://www.worldstandards.eu/electricity/plug-voltage-by-country/south-korea/) (checked 2026-11-03).

## 10. Emergency help and U.S. consular support

- **Police, ambulance, fire, and U.S. Embassy** — Call 112 for police and 119 for ambulance or fire. The Korea Tourism Organization’s 1330 line provides multilingual travel information and interpretation, but is not a replacement for emergency services. The U.S. State Department lists +82-2-397-4114 for the U.S. Embassy in Seoul, including after-hours emergencies. Check live contact details before travel.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-03.*

- Sources: [U.S. Department of State — South Korea emergency numbers and Embassy contacts](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/south-korea.html) (checked 2026-11-03); [Korea Tourism Organization — 1330 Korea Travel Helpline](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=187149) (checked 2026-11-03).
