# Bosnia and Herzegovina country guide

**Audience:** U.S. passport holders.  
**Facts checked:** 2026-11-05; individual dates appear with each fact.  
**Status:** Published facts, arranged in Japan’s ten-section guide format.  
**Formatted:** November 5, 2026.

Requirements can depend on trip purpose, length of stay, route, and personal circumstances. The conditions below identify facts that apply to a particular trip type. Regional, airport, and provider examples retain their stated scope. This collection does not provide a live forecast, exchange rate, or disruption feed.

## 1. Entry, passport, and stay type

- **Passport and permission checks** — Use a passport valid for three months after departure with space for a stamp. Bosnia and Herzegovina is outside Schengen; check its own entry and visitor-registration rules.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*
- **Short tourist stays** — U.S. tourist visits of up to 90 days do not require a visa under the checked country guidance. Confirm registration and counting rules before travel; this is separate from Schengen.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.* **Applies to:** purpose: tourism; trip length: 1–90 days.

- Sources: [U.S. Department of State — Bosnia and Herzegovina: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/bosnia-and-herzegovina.html) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 4. Currency, payments, and customs cash declaration

## 6. Health, care, and medicine

- **Health preparation for your itinerary** — Discuss hepatitis A and other itinerary-specific precautions with a clinician. Review routine vaccines, including measles protection. Any global CDC notice is not by itself a country-specific outbreak report. Arrange advice before travel; this summary does not prescribe treatment.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention — Bosnia and Herzegovina: Traveler View](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/destinations/traveler/none/bosnia-and-herzegovina) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 7. Local culture and respectful travel

- **Check visitor access at places of worship** — Places of worship can have visitor hours that differ from prayer times and change with the season. At Sarajevo’s Gazi Husrev-beg Mosque, visitor information and tickets are handled through the courtyard information office. Check the current arrangements before entering and follow staff instructions. This is a Sarajevo example; do not assume that another mosque, church, or religious site uses the same visiting rules.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Gazi Husrev-beg Waqf — Information for visitors](https://begovadzamija.ba/en/tourism/) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 8. Phones, internet, and staying connected

- **Check BH Telecom’s tourist SIM options** — BH Telecom offers tourist packages with physical SIM and eSIM options. Check your unlocked phone’s compatibility and ask the provider about activation, any identification requirement, package validity, and the areas on your route. Confirm whether your chosen package includes calls and texts or only the data you need. Buy through a listed sales point or the provider’s own channel, and keep a copy of the activation instructions.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [BH Telecom — Tourist offer](https://www.bhtelecom.ba/en/tourist-offer/) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 9. Electricity, plugs, and device preparation

- **Check the outlet and your device’s input label** — Bosnia and Herzegovina uses European-style Type C and F plugs. Electrical references give a 230 V, 50 Hz supply, while some visitor material still describes it as 220 V. Treat it as a higher-voltage supply than a standard U.S. outlet and confirm the actual outlet at your accommodation. A plug adapter changes the fit, not the voltage. Use a 120 V-only appliance only with conversion approved by its manufacturer.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Electrical Safety First — Bosnia and Herzegovina travel adaptors](https://www.electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk/safety-advice/travel-advice/bosnia-herzegovina/) (checked 2026-11-05); [State Electricity Regulatory Commission of Bosnia and Herzegovina — General electricity supply conditions: 400/230 V low-voltage network](https://www.derk.ba/DocumentsPDFs/Opsti-uslovi-za-isporuku-el-energije-BdBiH.pdf) (checked 2026-11-05); [Embassy of Bosnia and Herzegovina — Visit Bosnia and Herzegovina 2026: practical electricity information](https://www.bhembassy.org/data/images/Visit%20Bosnia%20and%20Herzegovina%202026.pdf) (checked 2026-11-05).
