# Saudi Arabia country guide

**Audience:** U.S. passport holders.  
**Facts checked:** 2026-11-05; individual dates appear with each fact.  
**Status:** Published facts, arranged in Japan’s ten-section guide format.  
**Formatted:** November 5, 2026.

Requirements can depend on trip purpose, length of stay, route, and personal circumstances. The conditions below identify facts that apply to a particular trip type. Regional, airport, and provider examples retain their stated scope. This collection does not provide a live forecast, exchange rate, or disruption feed.

## 1. Entry, passport, and stay type

- **Passport and permission checks** — A visa is required. Carry a passport valid for six months after arrival with two blank pages. Hajj requires its own authorized visa and permit; a tourist route is not a Hajj authorization.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [U.S. Department of State — Saudi Arabia: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/saudi-arabia.html) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 2. Getting there and border arrival

- **Declare cash and check restricted goods before packing** — Before entering Saudi Arabia, check whether any luggage, gifts, cash, or controlled items need a customs declaration. ZATCA provides an electronic declaration service and declaration offices at border ports. Its traveler guidance lists medicines and drones as examples of restricted items that may need approval. Keep supporting documents available and confirm current requirements with the responsible authority; a visa does not replace permission to bring a restricted item.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority — Guidelines for travelers: declaration thresholds](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/customs-individual/Travel-pages/disclosure-amounts.aspx) (checked 2026-11-05); [Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority — Traveler declaration procedures](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/customs-individual/Travel-pages/declare.aspx) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 3. Getting around the country

- **Riyadh metro and bus network** — Riyadh's development authority describes an integrated metro and bus network with interchange stations. Check the current route, operating status, and ticket rules. This information is specific to Riyadh.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Royal Commission for Riyadh City — Riyadh metro and bus network](https://www.rcrc.gov.sa/en/projects/king-abdulaziz-project-for-riyadh-public-transport-2/) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 4. Currency, payments, and customs cash declaration

- **Currency and payment planning** — Saudi riyal (SAR). Cards are used in cities, but U.S. cards may not work everywhere outside them; keep an appropriate payment backup.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [U.S. Department of State — Saudi Arabia: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/saudi-arabia.html) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 5. Safety, weather, and disruptions

- **U.S. advisory and regional precautions** — The U.S. advisory is Level 3, with Level 4 restrictions near Yemen. Regional armed-conflict risks can affect airports and assistance; read current embassy and airline updates. Advisory checked November 5, 2026; recheck before departure.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [U.S. Department of State — Saudi Arabia: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/saudi-arabia.html) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 6. Health, care, and medicine

- **Health preparation for your itinerary** — Malaria prevention depends on the area; pilgrimage health conditions are separate from general tourism advice. Ask a clinician and the responsible Saudi authority about the exact trip. Review routine vaccines, including measles protection. Any global CDC notice is not by itself a country-specific outbreak report. Arrange advice before travel; this summary does not prescribe treatment.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention — Saudi Arabia: Traveler View](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/destinations/traveler/none/saudi-arabia) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 7. Local culture and respectful travel

- **Public dress and behaviour have legal rules** — Saudi Arabia’s Public Decorum Charter applies in public places. Dress modestly and avoid clothing with images or messages that breach the rules. Do not treat these requirements as just a suggestion about local fashion. Check venue instructions as well, since a religious site or other facility may have additional entry conditions. The official charter explains the rules and the penalties for violations.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Saudi Ministry of Tourism e-Visa Portal — Public Decorum Charter](https://visa.visitsaudi.com/home/publicdecorum) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 8. Phones, internet, and staying connected

- **stc visitor SIM option** — stc describes Sawa Visitor SIM packages for travelers. Confirm identification, device compatibility, activation, current coverage, and the chosen plan's data and calling limits with stc. This fact does not fix a price or claim a particular plan is best.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [stc — stc visitor SIM option](https://www.stc.com.sa/content/stc/sa/en/personal/mobile/packages/sawa-ziyara.html) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 9. Electricity, plugs, and device preparation

- **Plugs, supply, and device compatibility** — Electrical Safety First lists Type G plugs, 220 V, and 60 Hz. Check each device's input label and the actual outlet. A plug adapter changes fit, not voltage. A 120 V-only appliance needs manufacturer-approved conversion or should be left at home. Saudi standards also discuss legacy 127 V arrangements; confirm the supply at the accommodation before using appliances.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Electrical Safety First — Saudi Arabia: travel adaptors](https://www.electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk/safety-advice/travel-advice/saudi-arabia/) (checked 2026-11-05); [Saudi Standards, Metrology and Quality Organization — Electrical plugs and sockets campaign](https://saso.gov.sa/en/consumer_awareness/awareness_campaigns/plugs_and_sockets_campaign/Pages/campagine_about.aspx) (checked 2026-11-05).

  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [U.S. Department of State — Saudi Arabia: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/saudi-arabia.html) (checked 2026-11-05).
