## 1. Entry, passport, and stay type

- **Passport and permission checks** — Use a passport valid for at least three months beyond planned Schengen departure, with two blank pages. Check the correct permission for employment, study, or residence.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*
- **Short tourism within the Schengen limit** — U.S. tourists may usually visit Schengen for 90 days in 180, counting all member countries together. Earlier visits count. Employment and longer stays need separate permission.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.* **Applies to:** purpose: tourism; trip length: 1–90 days.

- Sources: [U.S. Department of State — Austria: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/austria.html) (checked 2026-11-05); [U.S. Department of State — U.S. Travelers in Europe](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/planning/guidance/europe.html) (checked 2026-11-05).

## 7. Local culture and respectful travel

- **Tipping in Vienna is a custom, not a fixed bill** — In Vienna, tipping is customary in places such as restaurants, cafés, and taxis when you are satisfied with the service. Check the bill and decide what you want to leave rather than assuming a U.S.-style percentage is required. This is a Vienna example of a service custom; it is not a nationwide legal charge or a rule that every venue handles payment in the same way.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Vienna Tourist Board — Money needs and tipping](https://www.wien.info/en/travel-info/tourist-info/money-needs-352200) (checked 2026-11-05).

Check the current validity, included calls and data, and any roaming limits before buying. Coverage and continued use after the package ends depend on the plan’s terms.

## 9. Electricity, plugs, and device preparation

- **Plugs, supply, and device compatibility** — Electrical Safety First lists Type C/F plugs, 230 V, and 50 Hz. Check each device's input label and the actual outlet. A plug adapter changes fit, not voltage. A 120 V-only appliance needs manufacturer-approved conversion or should be left at home.  
  *Checked: 2026-11-05.*

- Sources: [Electrical Safety First — Austria: travel adaptors](https://www.electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk/safety-advice/travel-advice/austria/) (checked 2026-11-05).
