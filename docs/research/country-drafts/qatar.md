### Research Notes

**Supplied research note (unverified):** Checked 2026-10-14. • Advisory: Level 3, Reconsider Travel, for all of Qatar (reissued Aug 28, 2026) because of armed conflict. U.S.-Iran hostilities since Feb 28, 2026 bring drone and missile risk and flight disruption (State Dept). • UK FCDO dropped its advice against all but essential travel on Jun 18, 2026, but reports Iranian strikes in the region since Jul 8, 2026. Expect short-notice airspace closures (FCDO). • Entry: no visa needed in advance. State Dept (Oct 1, 2026) lists a visa on arrival, US$21, valid 90 days. Passport valid 6 months past departure with 2 blank pages; bring a return ticket. Emergency passports are refused (State Dept). • Conflict: Visit Qatar describes a 2-year multiple-entry visa waiver (90 days per stay), 3 months' passport validity and a hotel booking. Confirm with the Ministry of Interior before showing either version. • Money: Qatari riyal (QAR), pegged at 3.64 per US$1, so a fixed rate works. Declare cash of QAR 50,000 or more (QCB, FCDO). • Power: Type G (UK-style) sockets, 240 V / 50 Hz. U.S. plugs need an adapter (Visit Qatar). • Emergency: 999 for police, fire and ambulance. U.S. Embassy Doha +974 4496-6000, day and after hours (State Dept). • Health insurance: the MOPH visitor policy costs QAR 50 a month. Sources disagree on whether short trips need it (MOPH: mandatory; FCDO: stays over 30 days; Visit Qatar: recommended). • Medication: carry the prescription and a doctor's report in English or Arabic, under 6 months old. Controlled medicines may need an MOPH permit before travel (FCDO, Aug 19, 2026). Drug laws are zero-tolerance, even for trace amounts. • Laws: vaping is illegal; photographing people without consent can bring prison or a QAR 100,000 fine; drones need a permit (FCDO). Unpaid debts and bounced checks can lead to exit bans (OSAC). • Source problems: the State Dept Qatar page sometimes fails for bots (legacy URLs redirect to it). The MOI visa portal, MOPH site and Qatar's U.S. embassy site block bots. No GTFS or open transit data found.
