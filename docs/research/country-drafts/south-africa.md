### Research Notes

**Supplied research note (unverified):** Checked 2026-10-14. • Advisory: Level 2, Exercise Increased Caution (crime, terrorism, unrest, kidnapping), issued May 27, 2025. Take extra care in city centers after dark and in townships; avoid driving outside cities at night (State Dept). • Entry: no visa for stays under 90 days. Passport valid 30 days past departure with 2 consecutive completely blank visa pages, or you can be refused entry (State Dept). • New: the Electronic Travel Authorisation (eta.dha.gov.za) launched Aug 12, 2026. It's being phased in for visa-required nationalities and is optional for U.S. citizens; no official fee listed (Home Affairs). • New: since Jul 1, 2026, everyone entering or leaving must file a SARS online traveller declaration (web, SATMS app or QR code) within 24 hours before travel. SARS sets the cash limit at R100,000; older sources still say R25,000 (SARS). • Money: South African rand (ZAR). SARB publishes the daily reference rate (the page loads by JavaScript; no official API). Card skimming is common at ATMs (SARB, FCDO). • Power: 220/230 V, 50 Hz, round-pin plugs (15 A three-pin Type M and 5 A two-pin). Bring an adapter (South African Tourism). • Emergency: 10111 police, 10177 ambulance and fire, 112 from a mobile (FCDO). U.S. citizens should call the consulates: Johannesburg +27-11-290-3000, Cape Town +27-21-702-7300, Durban +27-31-310-3300. Embassy Pretoria +27-12-431-4000 (State Dept). • Health: malaria risk in low-lying Mpumalanga and Limpopo, including Kruger (antimalarials advised Sep to May); none in Cape Town or Johannesburg. Yellow fever certificate if arriving from a risk country (TravelHealthPro). • Getting around: avoid minibus taxis; book ride-hailing in the app and check the plate and driver ID. From Cape Town airport, stay on the M3/N2 and don't follow GPS off main roads (FCDO, 2026). • Power cuts: Eskom reported 300 days without load-shedding as of Mar 12, 2026, but local 'load reduction' cuts still happen (Eskom). • Source problems: Home Affairs' visa list is on the old home-affairs.gov.za domain; the Border Management Authority site blocks bots; no official GTFS for Gautrain, MyCiTi or Metrorail; gov.za's holiday list doesn't yet show the Nov 4, 2026 election holiday.
