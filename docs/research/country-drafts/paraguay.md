### Research Notes

**Supplied research note (unverified):** Checked 2026-10-14. • First-round pilot country. • Advisory: Level 1, issued Aug 19, 2026. Level 2 for Alto Paraná (incl. Ciudad del Este), Amambay, Canindeyú, Concepción and San Pedro because of crime (State Dept). • Entry: no visa for U.S. tourist or business stays up to 90 days. Passport valid 6 months past arrival, 1 blank page per stamp. An airport departure tax may already be in the ticket price (State Dept). • The visa waiver comes from Ley 7314/2024 and runs to Aug 13, 2027 unless renewed. Re-check before then (Migraciones). • Get the passport stamped at the point of entry, including land borders. Travelers without the entry stamp are fined on exit (FCDO). • Money: guaraní (PYG, ₲). Pull the BCP daily rate rather than hardcoding it. BCP has no API or CSV for daily rates. • Power: Type C plugs, 220 V / 50 Hz. U.S. plugs need an adapter, and 120 V-only devices need a converter (SENATUR). • Emergency: 911 police, 141 ambulance (SEME), 132 fire. U.S. Embassy +595-21-248-3000, after hours +595-21-229-581 (State Dept). • Health: dengue and chikungunya circulate (weekly MSPBS PDF reports). Yellow fever certificate only if arriving from Brazil, Bolivia, Peru or Venezuela (TravelHealthPro). • New in 2026: Line E3 electric bus runs between the airport and downtown Asunción, ₲3,400 fare, reduced pre-operational hours (MOPC). • Source problems: the Paraguayan consulate's Washington visa page is stale and still lists a US$160 visa on arrival, so don't use it. Most Paraguayan government sites are Spanish-only, and Migraciones, MSPBS and CDC block bots. No official source found for tourist SIM/eSIM rules.
