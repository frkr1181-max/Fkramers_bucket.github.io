### Research Notes

**Supplied research note (unverified):** Checked 2026-10-14. • Advisory: Level 3, Reconsider Travel (terrorism, Iranian and Houthi drone and missile threats, exit bans, social media laws), reissued Oct 12, 2026. Level 4, Do Not Travel, for the Yemen border region (State Dept). • UK FCDO (Oct 13, 2026) advises against all but essential travel to Riyadh, Aseer, Najran and Jizan provinces, Taif, Jeddah and Dammam, and every airport except Medina, after Houthi attacks since Jul 13, 2026 (FCDO). • Entry: visa required. Get the tourist eVisa in advance or a visa on arrival for a fee. Passport valid 6 months past arrival with 2 blank pages. Overstays are fined from SAR 100 a day (State Dept). • eVisa: valid 1 year, multiple entry, up to 90 days in total. SAR 300 plus mandatory medical insurance; confirm the total at checkout. Umrah is allowed on it outside Hajj season (Ministry of Tourism). • Money: Saudi riyal (SAR), pegged at 3.75 per US$1. The cash declaration threshold conflicts (State Dept SAR 40,000, ZATCA customs SAR 60,000); use ZATCA (SAMA, ZATCA). • Power: Type G (BS 1363) sockets at 220 to 230 V, 60 Hz. Some older buildings still have 127 V U.S.-style sockets (SASO, Saudi Electricity Co.). • Emergency: 911 (unified in Riyadh, Makkah and Eastern regions); 999 police, 997 ambulance, 998 fire, 937 Ministry of Health. U.S. Embassy Riyadh +966-11-835-4000; consulates Jeddah +966-12-220-5000, Dhahran +966-13-839-5700 (State Dept). • Health: no vaccine certificate needed when flying from the U.S.; Umrah and Hajj need a meningococcal ACWY certificate. MERS-CoV is still reported, so avoid camels and raw camel milk (TravelHealthPro). • Medication: many prescriptions need approval in advance through SFDA's Controlled Drugs System (online traveler permit since 2025), which can take weeks (SFDA, State Dept). • Laws: alcohol, cannabis and CBD are illegal. Posts criticizing the government or religion can lead to arrest. U.S. citizens were detained in 2026 for photographing missile interceptions. Public decency fines include SAR 1,000 for photographing people without permission (State Dept, Visit Saudi). • Dress: cover shoulders and knees; women don't need an abaya or headscarf (FCDO). • Source problems: visitsaudi.com and ksavisa.sa are JavaScript apps (need a headless browser or manual entry); the U.S. Embassy site blocks bots; the OSAC report (Jan 2025) predates the 2026 escalation.
