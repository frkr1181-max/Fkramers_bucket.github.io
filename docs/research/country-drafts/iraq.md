### Research Notes

**Supplied research note (unverified):** • Entry: U.S. tourists need a visa for Iraq. Federal Iraq requires an e-Visa before travel, while the Kurdistan Region has separate visa options. A Kurdistan-only visa does not authorize travel throughout federal Iraq. • Transportation: Flights, taxis, buses, and limited train services exist. Road travel is dangerous, public transportation is unreliable, and flights may be disrupted by security incidents. • Safety & Health: The U.S. State Department advises Level 4: Do Not Travel. Terrorism, kidnapping, armed conflict, limited healthcare, and emergency evacuation difficulties are major concerns. • Research Limitations: Some official sources are in Arabic or Kurdish, and the federal e-Visa portal requires JavaScript. Visa rules, transport schedules, security conditions, and mobile plans require frequent updates.
