### Research Notes

**Supplied research note (unverified):** • Entry: U.S. tourists can generally enter Senegal visa-free for up to 90 days. Passports should have at least 6 months of validity. Yellow fever vaccination requirements depend on travel history. • Transportation: Dakar has buses, BRT, taxis, rideshare, and regional trains. The TER now connects Dakar with Blaise Diagne International Airport. Intercity transportation varies in reliability. • Safety & Health: Pickpocketing, demonstrations, Casamance regional risks, malaria, and limited healthcare outside Dakar are important considerations. Travel medical insurance is recommended. • Research Limitations: Many official sources are in French, and some information is Dakar-specific. Entry rules, transport fares, mobile plans, vaccination guidance, and travel advisories need regular updates.
