### Research Notes

**Supplied research note (unverified):** • Entry: U.S. tourists can generally visit Germany visa-free for up to 90 days within a 180-day period. Passport validity, border documents, and ETIAS updates should be checked. • Transportation: ICE trains, regional trains, metros, trams, buses, and taxis are widely available. The Deutschland-Ticket covers eligible local and regional transportation but generally excludes long-distance ICE trains. • Safety & Health: Pickpocketing, public demonstrations, medication restrictions, and healthcare costs are important considerations. Travelers should carry some cash and consider travel medical insurance. • Research Limitations: Some official sources are in German, and certain information is Berlin-specific. Entry requirements, train fares, mobile plans, and travel advisories need regular updates.
