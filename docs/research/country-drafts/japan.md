### Research Notes

**Supplied research note (unverified):** Checked 2026-10-14. • Advisory: Level 1, Exercise Normal Precautions, issued May 15, 2025; travel guidance updated Sep 28, 2026 (State Dept). • Entry: no visa for tourist stays up to 90 days. Passport valid for the whole stay, 1 blank page per entry stamp. Declare cash of ¥1,000,000 or more. Visit Japan Web speeds up arrival forms (State Dept, MOFA). • JESTA, a paid online pre-travel authorization, must start by Mar 31, 2029 (date set by Cabinet Order). Not required as of Oct 2026 (Immigration Services Agency). • Medication: Adderall and some other U.S. prescriptions are illegal even with a prescription. Other medicines may need a Yunyu Kakunin-sho import certificate; apply at least 2 weeks ahead (State Dept, Embassy of Japan). • Money: yen (JPY). The Bank of Japan publishes daily USD/JPY, and its time-series API (JSON/CSV) launched Feb 18, 2026. Some clinics and small shops are cash-only. No tipping (BOJ, JNTO, State Dept). • Power: Type A plugs like the U.S., 100 V; 50 Hz in the east (Tokyo), 60 Hz in the west (Osaka, Kyoto). Three-prong plugs need an adapter (JNTO, KEK). • Emergency: 110 police, 119 fire and ambulance. Japan Visitor Hotline +81-50-3816-2787 (24/7, English). U.S. Embassy Tokyo +81-3-3224-5000 (JNTO, State Dept). • New in 2026: the departure tax rose to ¥3,000 on Jul 1, 2026 (Japan Tourism Agency). Japan Rail Pass prices rose Oct 1, 2026; ordinary 7-day pass ¥53,000 (JNTO). • From Nov 1, 2026, tax-free shopping becomes a refund system: pay tax in the store, get it back after a customs check when leaving. ¥5,000 minimum per store per day; keep goods in carry-on (Japan Tourism Agency). • Laws and customs: carry your passport at all times (¥100,000 fine for tourists without it). Tattoos are often barred at onsen, pools and gyms. Smoking only in designated areas (State Dept, JNTO). • Team note: the research plan assigns Japan to Hilna, but it sits on David's tab. Source problems: JR Pass, ODPT developer and JNTO Safety Tips pages not verified; JMA feeds and the holidays CSV are Japanese only.
