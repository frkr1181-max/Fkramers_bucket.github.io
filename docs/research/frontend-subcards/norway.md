## Entry & documents

### Visa

**Passport and permission checks** — Carry a passport valid for three months after departure with two blank pages. A 12-page U.S. emergency passport does not qualify for visa-free entry.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [U.S. Department of State — Norway: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/norway.html) (checked 2026-11-05); [U.S. Department of State — U.S. Travelers in Europe](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/planning/guidance/europe.html) (checked 2026-11-05)

**Short tourism within the Schengen limit** — U.S. tourists may usually visit Schengen for 90 days in 180, counting all member countries together. Earlier visits count. Employment and longer stays need separate permission.

Checked: 2026-11-05. Applies to: purpose: tourism; trip length: 1–90 days.

### Arrival forms

**Ask customs if you are unsure what to declare** — At Norwegian border crossings with red and green channels, use red for goods that need duty, tax, or a special permit. Use green only when your goods meet the allowance and other conditions. Walking through green is itself a declaration that you have nothing requiring customs clearance. If you are unsure, take the red channel and ask before leaving the customs area.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [Norwegian Customs — Red or green channel](https://www.toll.no/en/shopping-abroad/red-or-green-channel/) (checked 2026-11-05)

## Phones & tech

### eSIM

**Use a Telia shop if you do not have Norwegian digital ID** — Telia sells prepaid starter SIMs in Norway. Its online process uses BankID, but people without BankID or a Norwegian personal number can buy and register through a Telia shop. Ask which identity documents the shop needs before you go. Prepaid eSIM activation has extra ownership and BankID requirements, so confirm that route with customer service rather than assuming it works like the physical SIM. Check the current bundle and coverage for your trip.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [Telia Norway — Telia Kontant prepaid service](https://www.telia.no/mobilabonnement/kontantkort/) (checked 2026-11-05)

### Plugs & voltage

**Plugs, supply, and device compatibility** — Electrical Safety First lists Type C/F plugs, 230 V, and 50 Hz. Check each device's input label and the actual outlet. A plug adapter changes fit, not voltage. A 120 V-only appliance needs manufacturer-approved conversion or should be left at home.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [Electrical Safety First — Norway: travel adaptors](https://www.electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk/safety-advice/travel-advice/norway/) (checked 2026-11-05)
