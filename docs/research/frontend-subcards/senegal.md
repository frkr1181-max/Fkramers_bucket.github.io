### Plugs & voltage

**Plugs, supply, and device compatibility** — Electrical Safety First lists Type C/D/E/K plugs, 230 V, and 50 Hz. Check each device's input label and the actual outlet. A plug adapter changes fit, not voltage. A 120 V-only appliance needs manufacturer-approved conversion or should be left at home.

## Local culture

### Etiquette

**Let your host guide a shared meal** — Teranga is a Wolof word for hospitality, and shared meals are one way it is expressed in Senegal. If you are invited to eat with others, let your host explain how the meal will be served rather than assuming a single rule for every family. Ceebu jën, a rice-and-fish dish associated with Saint-Louis, is one important food tradition; recipes and serving practices vary.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [Embassy of Senegal in Qatar — Art and culture: Teranga](https://ambsenegal.qa/en/senegal/art-culture) (checked 2026-11-05); [UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage — Ceebu Jën, a culinary art of Senegal](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/ceebu-jen-a-culinary-art-of-senegal-01748) (checked 2026-11-05)
