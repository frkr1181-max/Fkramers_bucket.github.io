### Arrival forms

**Check personal baggage and cash declaration rules** — Qatar Customs exempts qualifying personal belongings and gifts only when they are personal, non-commercial, and within the stated value conditions; restricted goods remain subject to other laws. A separate declaration is required when carrying currency, bearer instruments, precious metals, or stones worth QAR 50,000 or more (or equivalent). Check the official rules before packing and ask Customs about anything unclear.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [Qatar General Authority of Customs — Materials authorized to enter and passenger exemptions](https://www.customs.gov.qa/English/PassengersIndividualsPortal/Pages/TravelersDeclaration.aspx) (checked 2026-11-05); [Qatar General Authority of Customs — Customs declaration of money transferred across borders](https://customs.gov.qa/English/PassengersIndividualsPortal/Pages/TravelersExemption.aspx) (checked 2026-11-05)

### Plugs & voltage

**Plugs, supply, and device compatibility** — Electrical Safety First lists Type G plugs, 240 V, and 50 Hz. Check each device's input label and the actual outlet. A plug adapter changes fit, not voltage. A 120 V-only appliance needs manufacturer-approved conversion or should be left at home.

Checked: 2026-11-05.
