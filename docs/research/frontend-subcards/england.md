## Entry & documents

### Visa

**Passport and permission checks** — For travel to England, carry a passport valid throughout the visit. U.S. visitors normally need UK electronic travel authorization or the appropriate visa; exemptions depend on immigration status and nationality.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [U.S. Department of State — England: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/united-kingdom.html) (checked 2026-11-05); [UK Home Office — Get an electronic travel authorisation](https://www.gov.uk/eta) (checked 2026-11-05)

**UK visitor authorization** — A U.S. tourist normally needs a UK ETA before departure unless exempt. It supports visits of up to six months, not an automatic right to enter. For work or a different purpose, check the appropriate permission.

Checked: 2026-11-05. Applies to: purpose: tourism.

Original section sources: [U.S. Department of State — England: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/united-kingdom.html) (checked 2026-11-05); [UK Home Office — Get an electronic travel authorisation](https://www.gov.uk/eta) (checked 2026-11-05)

## Phones & tech

### eSIM

**Check how you will get and activate a giffgaff SIM** — giffgaff offers SIMs for use in unlocked phones on its UK service. Its current delivery help says SIMs are mailed to UK addresses; it also lists local UK shops as a way to get a physical SIM. Do not assume one can be sent to your U.S. address. Check activation, eSIM availability for your account, package renewal, coverage, and calls back home before buying, and compare the total with your existing carrier’s roaming terms.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

### Plugs & voltage

**Plugs, supply, and device compatibility** — Electrical Safety First lists Type G plugs, 230 V, and 50 Hz. Check each device's input label and the actual outlet. A plug adapter changes fit, not voltage. A 120 V-only appliance needs manufacturer-approved conversion or should be left at home.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [Electrical Safety First — England: travel adaptors](https://www.electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk/safety-advice/travel-advice/united-kingdom/) (checked 2026-11-05)

## Local culture

### Etiquette

**Check the bill before adding a tip** — Tipping in England is not expected in every situation. Before leaving extra money at a restaurant, check whether the bill already includes a service charge. Follow the venue’s payment arrangements and leave a tip if you choose, rather than treating a familiar U.S. percentage as a requirement. This is guidance about a service custom, not a fixed charge that applies to every meal or business.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [VisitBritain — Useful information: money and tipping](https://www.visitbritain.com/en/plan-your-trip/useful-information) (checked 2026-11-05)
