## Entry & documents

### Visa

**Paid work needs the correct status** — A short visitor stay does not authorize paid work. Confirm the required Japanese visa and residence status with the responsible embassy or consulate before travel.

Checked: 2026-11-05. Applies to: purpose: work.

Original section sources: [Japan Ministry of Foreign Affairs — Visa information](https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/index.html) (checked 2026-11-05); [U.S. Department of State — Japan: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/japan.html) (checked 2026-11-05); [Embassy of Japan in the United States — Visa frequently asked questions](https://www.us.emb-japan.go.jp/itpr_en/visa-faq.html) (checked 2026-11-05)

**Passport and permission checks** — Carry a passport valid throughout the visit with a blank stamp page. A passport previously reported lost or stolen cannot be reused. Work and long-stay plans require the appropriate immigration permission.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [Japan Ministry of Foreign Affairs — Visa information](https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/index.html) (checked 2026-11-05); [U.S. Department of State — Japan: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/japan.html) (checked 2026-11-05); [Embassy of Japan in the United States — Visa frequently asked questions](https://www.us.emb-japan.go.jp/itpr_en/visa-faq.html) (checked 2026-11-05)

**Short tourist stays** — U.S. short-term tourism is visa-exempt for up to 90 days under Japanese visitor rules. Paid work is outside that visitor permission. Check the exact conditions with Japanese immigration.

Checked: 2026-11-05. Applies to: purpose: tourism; trip length: 1–90 days.

Original section sources: [Japan Ministry of Foreign Affairs — Visa information](https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/index.html) (checked 2026-11-05); [U.S. Department of State — Japan: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/japan.html) (checked 2026-11-05); [Embassy of Japan in the United States — Visa frequently asked questions](https://www.us.emb-japan.go.jp/itpr_en/visa-faq.html) (checked 2026-11-05)

**Stays beyond the short-visit allowance** — For a stay over 90 days, confirm the appropriate Japanese visa and residence status for the purpose. MOFA says a Certificate of Eligibility is normally obtained before the visa application; it is not a guarantee of visa issuance.

Checked: 2026-11-05. Applies to: trip length: 91 days or longer.

### Plugs & voltage

**Japan voltage and regional frequency** — Japan uses 100 V electricity. Frequency is 50 Hz in the east and 60 Hz in the west. Check device voltage, frequency, plug fit, and grounding; a plug adapter does not convert the supply. Follow the manufacturer's instructions.

Checked: 2026-11-05.
