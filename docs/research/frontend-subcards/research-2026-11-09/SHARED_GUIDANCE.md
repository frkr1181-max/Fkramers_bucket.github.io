# Shared preparation guidance

These notes apply generally to U.S. travelers. They do not establish a country’s specific medicine rules, insurance entry requirements or live inventory.

## medicine-preparation

Check each medicine’s active ingredient, amount and trip length with the destination’s embassy or medicines authority, including transit countries. A U.S. prescription does not prove import permission. Keep medicines in original labelled containers, carry prescription copies, and ask whether a permit is required.

**Scope:** General preparation for U.S. travelers; no specific drug, quantity or country import permission is established.

- [Traveling Abroad with Medicine | Travelers' Health | CDC](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/page/travel-abroad-with-medicine) — checked 2026-11-09; public page retrieval.

## insurance-check

Check whether your U.S. health insurance pays for overseas treatment. Consider separate medical and evacuation cover, and check exclusions for pre-existing conditions and planned activities. Trip-cancellation cover alone may not cover medical treatment. Ask how hospitals are paid and how to reach emergency assistance.

**Scope:** General coverage questions, not an insurance-product recommendation or a claim of entry compliance.

- [Travel Insurance | Travelers' Health | CDC](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/page/insurance) — checked 2026-11-09; public page retrieval.
