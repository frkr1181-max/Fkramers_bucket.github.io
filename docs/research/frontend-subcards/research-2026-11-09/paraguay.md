## Money & payments

### Tax-free shopping

**Outcome:** Source-backed draft answer. **Checked:** 2026-11-09.

Paraguay’s shopping-tourism regime applies reduced tax treatment to specified goods and registered sellers. It is not evidence of a universal airport cash-refund service. Confirm eligibility with the retailer.

**Limits:** Eligibility, goods, deadlines and operator fees must be checked for the actual purchase. No refund is guaranteed.
