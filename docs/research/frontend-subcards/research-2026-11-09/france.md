## Money & payments

### Tax-free shopping

**Outcome:** Source-backed draft answer. **Checked:** 2026-11-09.

Eligible non-EU residents can ask a participating retailer for a PABLO tax-refund form. Validate export when leaving the EU; the retailer or refund operator handles repayment.

**Limits:** Eligibility, goods, deadlines and operator fees must be checked for the actual purchase. No refund is guaranteed.

- [La détaxe en France pour les touristes - PABLO | Portail de la Direction Générale des Douanes et Droits Indirects](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/fiche/la-detaxe-en-france-pour-les-touristes-pablo) — checked 2026-11-09; public page retrieval.
