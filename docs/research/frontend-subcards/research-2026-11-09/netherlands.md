## Money & payments

### Tax-free shopping

**Outcome:** Source-backed draft answer. **Checked:** 2026-11-09.

Eligible non-EU residents can request VAT refunds on qualifying purchases of at least €50. Ask the retailer at purchase; the NL Customs VAT app supports export validation from 2026.

**Limits:** Eligibility, goods, deadlines and operator fees must be checked for the actual purchase. No refund is guaranteed.

- [How do I reclaim VAT on my purchases? | Dutch Customs](https://www.douane.nl/en/themes/travel-and-holiday/reclaiming-vat-on-export/) — checked 2026-11-09; public page retrieval.
