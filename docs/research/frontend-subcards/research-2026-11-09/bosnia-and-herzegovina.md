## Money & payments

### Tax-free shopping

**Outcome:** Source-backed draft answer. **Checked:** 2026-11-09.

A foreign buyer exporting eligible purchases can request the PDV-SL-2 form from the seller. Present the goods, invoices and completed form when leaving; exclusions apply.

**Limits:** Eligibility, goods, deadlines and operator fees must be checked for the actual purchase. No refund is guaranteed.
