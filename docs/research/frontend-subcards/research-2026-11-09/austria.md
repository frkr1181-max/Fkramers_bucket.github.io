## Money & payments

### Tax-free shopping

**Outcome:** Source-backed draft answer. **Checked:** 2026-11-09.

Non-EU residents can seek a VAT refund on qualifying purchases over €75. Keep the invoice and export paperwork, and obtain the required proof when leaving the EU.

**Limits:** Eligibility, goods, deadlines and operator fees must be checked for the actual purchase. No refund is guaranteed.

- [VAT Refund](https://www.bmf.gv.at/en/topics/customs/travellers/vat-refund.html) — checked 2026-11-09; public page retrieval.
