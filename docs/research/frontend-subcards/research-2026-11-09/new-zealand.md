### Tax-free shopping

**Outcome:** Source-backed draft answer. **Checked:** 2026-11-09.

New Zealand Customs says ordinary purchases do not qualify for a GST refund when you leave. Separate retailer export or airside-delivery arrangements can apply; ask before paying.

**Limits:** Eligibility, goods, deadlines and operator fees must be checked for the actual purchase. No refund is guaranteed.

- [Leaving New Zealand](https://www.customs.govt.nz/travel-to-and-from-new-zealand/leaving-new-zealand) — checked 2026-11-09; public page retrieval.
