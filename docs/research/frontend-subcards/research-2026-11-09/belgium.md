### Tax-free shopping

**Outcome:** Source-backed draft answer. **Checked:** 2026-11-09.

Eligible non-EU residents can seek a VAT refund for qualifying exported goods. Belgium’s minimum is more than €125 per invoice. Keep the goods and documents available for customs.

**Limits:** Eligibility, goods, deadlines and operator fees must be checked for the actual purchase. No refund is guaranteed.

- [Comment et quand obtenez-vous la restitution de la TVA lors de l’exportation de marchandises ? | SPF Finances](https://finances.belgium.be/fr/douanes_accises/particuliers/voyager/restitution-de-la-tva-%C3%A0-lexportation-de-biens) — checked 2026-11-09; public page retrieval.
