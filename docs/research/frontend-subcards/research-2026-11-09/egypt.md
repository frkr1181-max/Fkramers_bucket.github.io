## Money & payments

### Tax-free shopping

**Outcome:** Source-backed draft answer. **Checked:** 2026-11-09.

**Limits:** Eligibility, goods, deadlines and operator fees must be checked for the actual purchase. No refund is guaranteed.

**Outcome:** Precaution or preparedness guidance. **Checked:** 2026-11-10.

**Limits:** Scope is limited to the source and location described. No unverified price, timetable or individual eligibility is implied.
