# Follow-up research for the 380 unmatched subcards

**Completed:** November 10, 2026. **Research began:** November 9. **Audience:** U.S. passport holders.

All 380 previously unmatched slots across the 24 destinations were researched and received an explicit outcome: **356 draft content/guidance records**, **6 proposed design adaptations**, and **18 unresolved details**. These are not 380 newly published facts or 380 fully complete subcards.

## Content and verification limits

Directly supported summaries retain country, airport, city, operator and venue scope. Medicine preparation refers to each ingredient and quantity; it does not approve a drug for import. Insurance notes are questions to check, not insurance products or proof of entry compliance. A provider country list does not verify current inventory, shipping, rates or route coverage. A transport planning link does not establish a current fare, timetable or safe route.

FCDO and Canadian sources were used for local conditions, not to transfer British/Canadian passport rules, driving licences, health entitlements or consular services to U.S. visitors. No new foreign-nationality entry permission was inferred. Existing U.S. advisory content remains the place to start for travel-risk decisions.

The completed research includes source-page retrieval, source-specific text review and targeted official/provider web searches. A successful page download alone was not counted as a verified answer. Sources that failed, returned a generic landing page, or did not answer the question were not treated as proof. Search-result-only support is distinguished in source metadata; full-page verification remains necessary before publication of changing legal, tax, health or operational details.

No live forecast, exchange rate, provider price, specific medicine permission, or newly verified vaccination requirement was invented. No database, frontend implementation or publication flags were changed. All new records explicitly have `published: false`.

## Notable findings

- Japan’s tax-free shopping changed to a refund method for purchases from November 1, 2026; the new draft cites Japan Customs.
- Qatar’s tax authority says VAT has not been applied. Do not advertise a tourist VAT refund.
- New Zealand Customs says ordinary domestic purchases do not receive a departure GST refund.
- Great Britain’s luggage-based visitor VAT-refund scheme is not available; retailer export arrangements are separate.
- Senegal sources give different rainy-season month ranges. Its draft avoids selecting one range without further local confirmation.
- Sarajevo’s current FAQ confirms free Wi-Fi; older notices give different session limits. The draft does not invent a current allowance.
- Airport bus availability and safety advice can differ. Algeria’s transport notes preserve the Canadian caution about public buses.

## Research is not publication

The 18 unresolved records remain unavailable, with the reason recorded. The 82 general preparation records and other bounded outcomes must not be relabelled as complete country-specific answers. Human review should check the final wording, exact design labels, currency of each source and trip applicability before publication.
