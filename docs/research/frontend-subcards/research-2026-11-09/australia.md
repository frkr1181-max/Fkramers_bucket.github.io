## Money & payments

### Tax-free shopping

**Outcome:** Source-backed draft answer. **Checked:** 2026-11-09.

The Tourist Refund Scheme can refund GST or wine tax on eligible goods taken out of Australia. Check the current purchase, invoice and inspection requirements before buying.

**Limits:** Eligibility, goods, deadlines and operator fees must be checked for the actual purchase. No refund is guaranteed.

- [Tourist Refund Scheme (TRS)](https://www.abf.gov.au/entering-and-leaving-australia/tourist-refund-scheme) — checked 2026-11-09; public page retrieval.
