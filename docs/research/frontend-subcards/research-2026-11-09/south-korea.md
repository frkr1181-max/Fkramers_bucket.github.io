## Money & payments

### Tax-free shopping

**Outcome:** Source-backed draft answer. **Checked:** 2026-11-09.

Eligible visitors can receive immediate or later tax refunds at participating shops. Keep the passport, refund voucher and goods available for the applicable departure process.

**Limits:** Eligibility, goods, deadlines and operator fees must be checked for the actual purchase. No refund is guaranteed.

- [Comprehensive Tax Refund Guide-  VISITKOREA](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoBscView.do?vcontsId=248765) — checked 2026-11-09; public page retrieval.

## Local culture

### Local laws

**Outcome:** Source-backed draft answer. **Checked:** 2026-11-09.

Carry identification. Drug offences, including small amounts for personal use, can lead to imprisonment and fines.

**Limits:** General visitor preparation; no UK-only passport, licence or healthcare eligibility is transferred to U.S. travelers.
