### Tax-free shopping

**Outcome:** Source-backed draft answer. **Checked:** 2026-11-09.

Eligible foreign visitors can claim VAT on qualifying goods exported at departure. Keep tax invoices and present the goods for the required inspection before checking luggage.

**Limits:** Eligibility, goods, deadlines and operator fees must be checked for the actual purchase. No refund is guaranteed.
