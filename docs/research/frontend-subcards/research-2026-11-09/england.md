## Money & payments

### Tax-free shopping

**Outcome:** Source-backed draft answer. **Checked:** 2026-11-09.

Great Britain does not offer the ordinary visitor VAT-refund process for purchases carried home in luggage. Tax-free treatment may be available when the retailer ships goods directly abroad.

**Limits:** Eligibility, goods, deadlines and operator fees must be checked for the actual purchase. No refund is guaranteed.

- [Tax on shopping and services: Tax-free shopping - GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/tax-on-shopping/taxfree-shopping) — checked 2026-11-09; public page retrieval.

### Solo travel

**Outcome:** Precaution or preparedness guidance. **Checked:** 2026-11-10.

In London, hail a licensed black taxi or prebook a minicab through a licensed operator. Do not enter an unbooked minicab offered on the street.

**Limits:** Only the described location and topic are established. Recheck changing service details before travel.
