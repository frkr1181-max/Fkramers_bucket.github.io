### Tipping

**Outcome:** Custom or venue rule; retain its local scope. **Checked:** 2026-11-10.

Tipping is optional in Argentina. The tourism board describes about 10% as a common restaurant custom for good service. Check how the venue accepts tips.

**Limits:** Scope is limited to the source and location described. No unverified price, timetable or individual eligibility is implied.
