## Money & payments

### Tax-free shopping

**Outcome:** Source-backed draft answer. **Checked:** 2026-11-09.

For purchases from November 1, 2026, eligible shoppers pay the tax-inclusive price. Refund follows departure customs confirmation that the goods leave Japan. Keep goods and purchase records available.

**Limits:** Eligibility, goods, deadlines and operator fees must be checked for the actual purchase. No refund is guaranteed.

- [Tax Exemption (Refund) Program for Foreign Visitors : Japan Customs](https://www.customs.go.jp/kaigairyoko/taxrefund.htm) — checked 2026-11-09; public page retrieval.

## Local culture

### Tipping

**Outcome:** Custom or venue rule; retain its local scope. **Checked:** 2026-11-09.

Tipping is generally not expected in restaurants, taxis and hotels. Some private guides may accept a discreet gratuity; ask about the arrangement rather than applying a fixed percentage.

**Limits:** A custom, not a mandatory charge. Check the actual bill and venue policy.

- [Tipping in Japan | Travel Japan | JNTO](https://www.japan.travel/en/plan/tipping-in-japan/) — checked 2026-11-09; public page retrieval.
