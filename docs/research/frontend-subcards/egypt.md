## Phones & tech

### eSIM

**Ask about Vodafone’s tourist line before buying** — Vodafone Egypt advertises a Tourist Line for visitors. Use an official shop to check the current package and the documents needed to activate it with your passport. Confirm whether the offer is a physical SIM or an eSIM, whether your phone is compatible, and what happens if your visit is extended. Ask separately about calls to the United States, data limits, and coverage; an advertised tourist offer does not confirm every device or visitor’s eligibility.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [Vodafone Egypt — Tourist Line](https://web.vodafone.com.eg//en/tourist-line) (checked 2026-11-05)

### Plugs & voltage

**Plugs, supply, and device compatibility** — Electrical Safety First lists Type C/F plugs, 220 V, and 50 Hz. Check each device's input label and the actual outlet. A plug adapter changes fit, not voltage. A 120 V-only appliance needs manufacturer-approved conversion or should be left at home.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [Electrical Safety First — Egypt: travel adaptors](https://www.electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk/safety-advice/travel-advice/egypt/) (checked 2026-11-05)

### Vaccines

**Health preparation for your itinerary** — Discuss hepatitis A and food/water precautions. Yellow-fever entry proof can depend on travel from other countries, even when direct U.S. arrivals do not need it. Review routine vaccines, including measles protection. Any global CDC notice is not by itself a country-specific outbreak report. Arrange advice before travel; this summary does not prescribe treatment.

### Arrival steps

**Have your e-Visa paperwork ready** — If you enter Egypt with an e-Visa, print the approved visa and keep it with your passport. Have your travel itinerary and accommodation details available. The e-Visa portal also lists supporting letters for business or family visits. Approval of the e-Visa does not guarantee admission, so answer the arrival officer’s questions and carry the documents that support the purpose of your visit.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

## Safety

### Travel advisory

**U.S. advisory and regional precautions** — The U.S. advisory is Level 2. Northern and Middle Sinai, border military zones, and Western Desert travel outside licensed tours have stronger restrictions. Read the regional warnings. Advisory checked November 5, 2026; recheck before departure.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [U.S. Department of State — Egypt: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/egypt.html) (checked 2026-11-05)

### Emergency numbers

**Emergency help and U.S. assistance** — For an ambulance, call 123. For U.S. consular help after contacting local responders, the Embassy Cairo number is +20-2-2797-3300. Local services handle immediate emergencies; use the linked U.S. country page for current consular contacts.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [U.S. Department of State — Egypt: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/egypt.html) (checked 2026-11-05)
