### Plugs & voltage

**Plugs, supply, and device compatibility** — Electrical Safety First lists Type C/M/N plugs, 230 V, and 50 Hz. Check each device's input label and the actual outlet. A plug adapter changes fit, not voltage. A 120 V-only appliance needs manufacturer-approved conversion or should be left at home.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

## Local culture

### Etiquette

**Expect different languages across communities** — South Africa has 12 official languages, including South African Sign Language. Do not assume that everyone has the same first language or prefers to communicate in English. Ask what works for the person helping you, and keep an address or place name written down if pronunciation is getting in the way. Language and cultural practices vary by community; one city’s habits do not describe the whole country.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [South African Government — People of South Africa](https://www.gov.za/about-sa/south-africas-people) (checked 2026-11-05)
