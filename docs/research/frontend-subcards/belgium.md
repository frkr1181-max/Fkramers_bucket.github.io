## Entry & documents

### Visa

**Passport and permission checks** — Carry a passport valid for three months after leaving Schengen, with two blank pages. A 12-page U.S. emergency passport does not qualify for visa-free Belgian entry.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [U.S. Department of State — Belgium: travel advisory and country guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/belgium.html) (checked 2026-11-05); [U.S. Department of State — U.S. Travelers in Europe](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/planning/guidance/europe.html) (checked 2026-11-05)

**Short tourism within the Schengen limit** — U.S. tourists may usually visit Schengen for 90 days in 180, counting all member countries together. Earlier visits count. Employment and longer stays need separate permission.

Checked: 2026-11-05. Applies to: purpose: tourism; trip length: 1–90 days.

### Arrival forms

**Check whether your private stay needs a declaration** — If you are a non-EU national staying under three months in a private residence in Belgium, check the arrival-reporting instructions for your municipality. Belgian guidance provides the My Address in Belgium online form; the City of Brussels says to report within three working days. Hotel or lodging-house stays, including holiday rentals, and hospital or prison stays are exempt under the Brussels guidance. Keep the confirmation, and remember this does not extend your permitted stay.

Checked: 2026-11-05. Applies to: trip length: 1–90 days.

Original section sources: [Belgian Federal Public Service Foreign Affairs — Travel to Belgium: short-stay reporting](https://oeganda.diplomatie.belgium.be/en/travel-belgium) (checked 2026-11-05); [City of Brussels — Report a short stay in Brussels](https://www.brussels.be/non-eu-citizen-report-short-stay-belgium-declaration-arrival) (checked 2026-11-05)

## Phones & tech

### eSIM

**Arrange prepaid identification with the provider** — Proximus offers Pay&Go prepaid service, but the SIM must be identified before use. Its terms list an international passport among the accepted documents for in-person identification. Bring your passport to an official sales point and confirm what is needed for your situation. Ask about physical SIM versus eSIM availability, the current bundle, calls and texts, hotspot use, and coverage before buying rather than assuming an online activation route will work for every foreign visitor.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [Proximus — Specific terms for mobile service and prepaid identification](https://www.proximus.be/dam/jcr%3Aad10d2cc-42c7-4354-871e-7cd3ea9ea18b/cdn/sites/iportal/documents/pdfs/res/special-terms-and-conditions-mobile-phone-v3-rev-new-telco.pdf) (checked 2026-11-05)

### Plugs & voltage

**Plugs, supply, and device compatibility** — Electrical Safety First lists Type C/E plugs, 230 V, and 50 Hz. Check each device's input label and the actual outlet. A plug adapter changes fit, not voltage. A 120 V-only appliance needs manufacturer-approved conversion or should be left at home.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [Electrical Safety First — Belgium: travel adaptors](https://www.electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk/safety-advice/travel-advice/belgium/) (checked 2026-11-05)

## Getting around

### Subway

**Use SNCB-NMBS for train planning** — The SNCB-NMBS website and app provide train times, service information, and tickets. The route planner can also include bus, tram, and metro connections, but a suggested connection does not mean that one train ticket covers every leg. Check each ticket before boarding and keep the ticket available for inspection. The operator links to its current iOS and Android app information.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [SNCB-NMBS — SNCB-NMBS route planner](https://www.belgiantrain.be/en/travel-info/prepare-for-your-journey/plan-your-journey-with-the-route-planner) (checked 2026-11-05); [SNCB-NMBS — Buying through the SNCB-NMBS app](https://www.belgiantrain.be/en/support/faq/faq-purchase-payment/faq-purchase-app) (checked 2026-11-05)

## Local culture

### Etiquette

**Expect more than one language and place name** — Belgium has three national languages: Dutch, French, and German. Some places, especially in Brussels, have names in both French and Dutch. Check the station or street name carefully when matching a booking to a map. Ask which language works for the person helping you rather than assuming that everyone uses the same one.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [Belgian Federal Government — Languages and communities in Belgium](https://www.belgium.be/en/education/coming_to_study_in_belgium) (checked 2026-11-05); [European Commission — Country Compendium: Belgium](https://commission.europa.eu/system/files/2023-11/styleguide_english_dgt_country_compendium_en.pdf) (checked 2026-11-05)
