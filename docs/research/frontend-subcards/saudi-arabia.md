## Entry & documents

### Visa

**Passport and permission checks** — A visa is required. Carry a passport valid for six months after arrival with two blank pages. Hajj requires its own authorized visa and permit; a tourist route is not a Hajj authorization.

### Arrival forms

**Declare cash and check restricted goods before packing** — Before entering Saudi Arabia, check whether any luggage, gifts, cash, or controlled items need a customs declaration. ZATCA provides an electronic declaration service and declaration offices at border ports. Its traveler guidance lists medicines and drones as examples of restricted items that may need approval. Keep supporting documents available and confirm current requirements with the responsible authority; a visa does not replace permission to bring a restricted item.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority — Guidelines for travelers: declaration thresholds](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/customs-individual/Travel-pages/disclosure-amounts.aspx) (checked 2026-11-05); [Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority — Traveler declaration procedures](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/customs-individual/Travel-pages/declare.aspx) (checked 2026-11-05)

### Plugs & voltage

**Plugs, supply, and device compatibility** — Electrical Safety First lists Type G plugs, 220 V, and 60 Hz. Check each device's input label and the actual outlet. A plug adapter changes fit, not voltage. A 120 V-only appliance needs manufacturer-approved conversion or should be left at home. Saudi standards also discuss legacy 127 V arrangements; confirm the supply at the accommodation before using appliances.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [Electrical Safety First — Saudi Arabia: travel adaptors](https://www.electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk/safety-advice/travel-advice/saudi-arabia/) (checked 2026-11-05); [Saudi Standards, Metrology and Quality Organization — Electrical plugs and sockets campaign](https://saso.gov.sa/en/consumer_awareness/awareness_campaigns/plugs_and_sockets_campaign/Pages/campagine_about.aspx) (checked 2026-11-05)

## Local culture

### Etiquette

**Public dress and behaviour have legal rules** — Saudi Arabia’s Public Decorum Charter applies in public places. Dress modestly and avoid clothing with images or messages that breach the rules. Do not treat these requirements as just a suggestion about local fashion. Check venue instructions as well, since a religious site or other facility may have additional entry conditions. The official charter explains the rules and the penalties for violations.

Checked: 2026-11-05.

Original section sources: [Saudi Ministry of Tourism e-Visa Portal — Public Decorum Charter](https://visa.visitsaudi.com/home/publicdecorum) (checked 2026-11-05)
