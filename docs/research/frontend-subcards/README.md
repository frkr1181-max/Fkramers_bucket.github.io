# Frontend subcard research pack

> **Follow-up completed November 10, 2026:** [Read the research for all 380 original gaps](research-2026-11-09/README.md): 356 draft content/guidance records, 6 proposed design adaptations, and 18 unresolved details. The counts below describe the original keyword-matching baseline, not current completeness.

Prepared November 9, 2026 for the 24 RoamReady destinations.

## What is included

- 263 existing published-guide facts, retaining wording, dates, applicability text, and original section sources.
- Eight main guides, with 32 subcard labels confirmed from the Figma design; 768 country/subcard combinations.
- 388 combinations have matching supporting information; 380 need additional research. A match is partial evidence, not proof of completeness.
- Three new official-source search notes for Narita and Hamad Airport; these remain drafts pending full page review.

## Important limits

The newer `origin/hifi-site` branch documents four hand-written topics per guide. The exact 32 Learn more frame labels were read from the Figma High-fidelity Layout page. See [the design inventory](DESIGN_SUBCARDS.md). Japan-specific labels are adapted explicitly for country-level research; those adaptations are proposals, not design edits. Keyword matching organizes the existing facts; it cannot establish that the content fully answers a subcard. Repeated facts intentionally retain their original wording and conditions.

Existing facts were checked November 3–5, 2026, as recorded in the original guides; organizing them today does not reverify them. Source-bank links are research leads only. No source-bank claims have been promoted to published facts. This pack does not modify the frontend, database, or publication status.

## Remaining research

Prioritize subcards with no matching evidence. Review matched cards for missing details too: airport names do not answer border procedures; a currency name does not answer ATM fees; insurance mentions do not answer pharmacy access; travel advisories do not necessarily answer solo travel questions. Verify changing entry/health/safety information before display. Transport details need airport/city scope. No current fees, schedules, forecasts, exchange rates, or provider prices were inferred.
