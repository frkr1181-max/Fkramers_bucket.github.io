# Original research integrated into saved country guides

The public historical research accordion has been removed. Normal guide cards and topic pages use the saved published facts, with their source links, dates and trip conditions. Original project research files have not been deleted or edited by this integration.

The supplied research collection covers **24 destinations and 240 sections**. All 240 sections map to saved published facts in their corresponding country/category; see `research-integration.json`. This file preserves each original note, paragraph, date and source list alongside the saved fact IDs and titles. It is a provenance mapping, not a claim that every sentence in an old draft is current or approved. The November 5 and November 10 publication records document the reviewed claim sets and prior decisions.

The current saved collection contains **316 published facts**, matching the live database export used for this comparison. Eleven already-published South Korea facts were missing from the previous static fallback; `previously-published-facts.json` preserves those records and the snapshot builder now includes them. Japan's original 100 V / east–west frequency detail is explicitly included in its electricity topic alongside device-compatibility guidance. Overlapping older facts remain saved without duplicating them across Japan's topic cards.

No new database facts, verification dates, updates, deletions, accounts, trips, schema or policies were created or changed for this integration. Inaccurate or unsupported historical wording is not copied into traveler guidance merely because it appeared in an old note. Twelve other catalog destinations have no supplied country research in this collection; they remain honestly empty rather than borrowing another country's data. They are listed in the integration file.

Rebuild:

```
python3 site/tools/build_snapshot.py
python3 site/tools/build_japan_subcards.py
python3 site/tools/build_research_integration.py
```

Verification: frontend integration, topic, Japan mapping and research integration checks; all 27 backend tests; exact comparison of the 316 snapshot facts to the live published export, preserving summaries, dates, sources and trip conditions.

## Focused topic pages for the other destinations

All 23 other researched destinations now use the same card and full-page navigation as Japan. The 35 other catalog destinations have a mapping structure, including the twelve without supplied research. Country-specific passages are derived from the saved published facts, preserving exact text, dates, sources and trip eligibility. A passage is placed once per guide. General or mixed transport material remains in a clearly titled Planning overview instead of being copied to multiple cards. No Japan-only airport, rail-pass or cultural labels are applied to other countries.

The mappings are generated by `site/tools/build_country_topics.py` into `site/data/country-topics.json`. They are presentation mappings, not newly reviewed facts or database writes. When a live fact changes, its full updated wording remains available in Planning overview until the mapping is regenerated; stale excerpts are not shown. Previous fact-ID topic links remain supported.

Available facts are connected; the project does not yet have Japan's depth of researched content for every country/subtopic. `country-topic-coverage.json` explicitly lists topics with and without published support. Empty topics use an unavailable state and cannot borrow unrelated material. The twelve unresearched catalog destinations retain their empty guide state.

Every saved non-Japan fact is reachable, sources/dates/conditions are unchanged, no passage is duplicated within a guide, and facts from a different country cannot leak in. Live five-day-tourism API checks passed for all 23 additional researched destinations (`country-api-verification.json`).
