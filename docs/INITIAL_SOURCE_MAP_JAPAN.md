# Initial source map: Japan

**Audience:** U.S. passport holders  
**Reviewed for source discovery:** October 27, 2026  
**Status:** Source leads only. No traveler guidance in this file is approved for publication.

Japan is the current frontend demo destination, so use it to prove the research-to-review workflow before copying the process across the other 23 countries. These are starting points gathered from the project topics; reviewers still need to open the pages and capture the exact claim, applicable purpose/stay, effective date, and checked date.

| Project topic | Source lead | What to verify before writing guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |

Link to current embassy/consulate contact details and state what consular services can and cannot do. |

## First review batch

Prioritize entry conditions, U.S. travel advisory, health notices, and emergency contacts. These are high-impact and can change; a reviewer should approve each one before it appears in a demo guide. Follow with the stable practical topics. Do not use the U.S. State Department advisory as the sole authority for Japanese immigration law; confirm entry conditions with Japan's immigration or foreign ministry sources.

For every topic, keep the page as an unapproved candidate until a reviewer writes a concise fact, attaches the direct source, records the source's jurisdiction and applicable conditions, and dates the check. The source page changing should create a review task rather than silently replace approved guidance.
